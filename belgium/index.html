Every figure on this page is computed by the Axiom rules engine executing statute-grounded encodings from rulespec-be (each parameter carries a proof span from the source text), over the Microcosm-BE population — calibrated against Chronicle targets, sums of published administrative facts: 254 targets in v0.5, 242 within 5%, every vintage and aging factor declared on the calibration dashboard. Encodings are validated case by case against EUROMOD BE_2025 per the axiom-oracles scoreboard — 23 in-scope policies, residuals attributed — and this prototype exists for exactly that kind of cross-model comparison.
